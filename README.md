# Ride-Hailing Analysis

## Overview
This project focuses on analyzing **ride-hailing data** to understand demand-supply dynamics, revenue patterns, and operational factors affecting ride services like Uber. The goal is to generate actionable insights to inform **pricing strategies, operational planning, and service optimization**.

## Key Objectives
- Analyze **demand and supply trends** across different times, locations, and conditions.  
- Identify **drivers of ride cancellations** to reduce service disruptions.  
- Examine **revenue patterns** and opportunities for optimization.  
- Assess the **impact of weather and external factors** on ride demand and performance.

## Features & Methodology
- **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Preparing ride logs for analysis.  
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Identifying patterns in demand, supply, and cancellations.  
- **Revenue Analysis:** Evaluating ride fares and revenue trends.  
- **Correlation Analysis:** Understanding the effect of weather and other variables on rides.  
- **Visualization:** Creating dashboards and charts to convey insights effectively.

## Technologies Used
- **Python** – Data analysis and visualization  
- **Pandas & NumPy** – Data manipulation  
- **Matplotlib & Seaborn** – Visualizations  
- Optional: **Jupyter Notebook** for interactive analysis

## Impact
The analysis helps ride-hailing platforms make **data-driven decisions** for pricing, operational efficiency, and customer satisfaction, while minimizing cancellations and maximizing revenue.
